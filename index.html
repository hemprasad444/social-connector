Social Connector

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Social Connector is a personal, self-hosted tool that lets its owner publish videos to, and manage comments on, their own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts from their own computer.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its developer to manage the developer's own accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads videos the owner has made to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Edits titles, descriptions, tags and privacy settings of the owner's videos.
  • Reads, replies to and moderates comments on the owner's videos.
  • Shows view and engagement statistics for the owner's channel.

See the Privacy Policy for how data is handled.
