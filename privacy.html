Social Connector is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's computer. There is no server, no website backend and no third-party service in between.

Data we access

When you sign in with Google, Social Connector asks permission to upload and manage videos, manage comments, and read analytics for your YouTube channel. It uses this access only to carry out actions you ask for.

How data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your own computer, in a file readable only by your user account.
  • Video files, comments and statistics are read from or sent to YouTube directly from your computer. They are not copied, stored or sent anywhere else.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services

Social Connector's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services and is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

You can remove Social Connector's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the local token file from your computer.
